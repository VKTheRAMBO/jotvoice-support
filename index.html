JotVoice support

JotVoice is a voice notebook for Apple Watch. Open it and it is already recording. Stop talking and the note saves itself. Your iPhone turns the recording into text on the device, adds where you were and the meeting you were in, and files reminders and shopping items into Apple Reminders.

Getting started

  • Install both apps. JotVoice records on Apple Watch and transcribes on the paired iPhone. Install JotVoice on your iPhone, then on your watch from the Watch app.
  • Open the iPhone app once. Allow speech recognition so your notes can become text. Reminders, Calendar and Notifications are optional.
  • Add the complication. Long-press your watch face, tap Edit, and add JotVoice to a corner or slot. One tap opens it already recording.
  • Use Siri or the Action Button. Say “Start a note in JotVoice”. On Apple Watch Ultra, set the Action Button to the “Jot a thought” shortcut in the Shortcuts app.

Hands-free recording

JotVoice waits until you have actually spoken, then saves once you have been quiet for the pause you choose (2, 4 or 7 seconds, or Off). A green ring shows the countdown; keep talking and it cancels. You can also turn on “Save when wrist is lowered” in the watch Settings.

Transcripts

  • Transcripts are made on your iPhone. When the iPhone is not nearby, notes wait on the watch and are sent when it is back in range.
  • To get text without your iPhone, use Dictate text on the watch.
  • If a note says “Transcript unavailable”, open it on the watch and tap Transcribe again.
  • To change the language, open Settings in the iPhone app and pick a language under Transcription.

Places and meetings

Allow location on the watch the first time it asks, and each note remembers where you recorded it. The Places tab on iPhone shows your notes on a map. With Calendar allowed, a note recorded during an event is labelled with that event.

JotVoice Pro

Recording, playback, places and iCloud sync are free, with 15 transcripts a month. JotVoice Pro adds unlimited transcripts, automatic Reminders and shopping list, “Remind me when I'm back here”, Ask your notes and the evening digest. Pro is a monthly subscription ($2.99), or a yearly subscription ($19.99) that starts with a free week. Prices may vary by country. Manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple ID subscriptions. Changed phones? Tap Restore purchases in Settings.

Sync and backup

Notes stay in step between your watch and iPhone automatically. Turn on Sync with iCloud in Settings to keep a copy in your private iCloud, for example when you change phones.
