JotVoice privacy policy

Effective 18 November 2026 · WorldLamp Apps · worldlampapps@gmail.com

The short version

JotVoice records voice notes on your Apple Watch and turns them into text on your iPhone. Your recordings, transcripts and the context around them stay on your devices, and in your own private iCloud if you turn sync on. We do not run servers for this app, there are no accounts, and we do not collect any data about you.

What stays on your devices

  • Recordings and transcripts. Saved on your watch and iPhone, and sent between them over Apple's WatchConnectivity. Deleting a note on either device deletes it on both.
  • Location. If you allow it, the watch takes one approximate location when you record a note. It is stored with that note. Your iPhone looks up a place name for it using Apple Maps. It is not used for anything else.
  • Calendar. If you allow it, the iPhone reads the event happening when a note was recorded, to label the note. JotVoice never changes your calendar.
  • Reminders. If you allow it, JotVoice adds items to Apple Reminders when a note asks to be reminded or lists things to buy, or when you tap a reminder action. It reads only the open items on its shopping list, to avoid duplicates.
  • Settings and counters. Your preferences and the number of free transcripts used this month are saved on your devices.

Speech recognition

Transcripts are made with Apple's speech recognition on your iPhone. Where on-device recognition is not available for your language or iPhone, Apple's speech recognition service may process the audio to make the transcript, under Apple's privacy policy. We never receive your audio or text.

Ask your notes and summaries

These features use Apple's on-device language model on your iPhone. Your notes are not sent to us or to any other company.

iCloud sync (optional)

If you turn on Sync with iCloud, your notes and recordings are stored in your private iCloud database, which only you can access. We cannot see it. Turn sync off at any time in Settings.

Purchases

JotVoice Pro is sold and billed by Apple. We never see your payment details. The app only learns whether Pro is active.

Analytics, ads and tracking

JotVoice has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not use third-party SDKs.

Deleting your data

Delete a note in the app to remove it from your watch, iPhone and iCloud. Deleting the apps removes everything stored on the devices. iCloud data can also be removed in your iPhone's Settings under your name, iCloud, Manage Storage.

Children

JotVoice is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Questions: worldlampapps@gmail.com.
